## Full Description

**Discover Homemade Food from Real Home Kitchens**

MealIn connects you with talented home cooks in your neighborhood who prepare fresh, delicious homemade meals. Skip the restaurant and enjoy authentic, home-cooked food delivered right to your doorstep.

**Why Choose MealIn?**

🍽️ **Authentic Home Cooking** — Every meal is prepared by real home cooks, not restaurants. Taste the difference of food made with love in actual home kitchens.

🏠 **Support Local Kitchens** — Help talented home cooks in your community earn from their culinary skills. Every order supports a local entrepreneur.

🛵 **Fast Delivery** — Get your favorite homemade meals delivered quickly to your address. Track your delivery in real-time on the map.

📍 **Find Kitchens Near You** — Discover home kitchens in your neighborhood. Browse menus, read reviews, and find new favorite dishes.

🗣️ **Voice Ordering** — Order hands-free! Just say "Hi MEAL" and speak your order. Our AI voice assistant understands natural speech.

💳 **Secure Payments** — Pay safely through Razorpay with UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and wallets.

🔔 **Smart Notifications** — Get instant updates when your order is accepted, being prepared, out for delivery, and delivered.

📅 **Reorder Favorites** — Easily reorder your past favorites with one tap. Your order history makes it simple.

**How It Works:**

1. **Browse** — Explore home kitchens and menus near you
2. **Order** — Select your favorite dishes or use voice ordering
3. **Pay** — Complete payment securely through Razorpay
4. **Track** — Follow your order and delivery in real-time
5. **Enjoy** — Receive fresh, homemade food at your doorstep

**Features:**

• Browse home kitchen menus with photos and prices
• Voice-powered ordering — just speak your order
• Real-time order tracking with live map
• Delivery partner tracking — see your rider on the map
• Order history and one-tap reorder
• Push notifications for order updates
• Multiple delivery addresses
• Rate and review kitchens and dishes
• In-app chat support
• Secure payment via Razorpay

**MealIn — Connecting Home Kitchens with You**

Every meal on MealIn is prepared by a real home cook in their own kitchen. We believe in empowering home entrepreneurs while bringing authentic, homemade food to your table.

Download MealIn today and taste the difference of real home cooking!
