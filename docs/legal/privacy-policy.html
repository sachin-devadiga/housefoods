Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Welcome to MealIn ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications MealIn, MealIn Resto and MealIn Rider (together, the "Apps"). We operate under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and delivery address when you create an account or place an order. Phone number is also verified by one-time password (OTP) sent by email.
  • Location Data: Precise location, collected with your permission while the App is in use, to find nearby home kitchens, assign a delivery partner, and track an order in progress. Background location is not collected. Riders' live location is shared with the customer for the duration of a delivery so the order can be tracked.
  • Order History: Details of orders you place, including items ordered, prices, delivery addresses, and any meal-plan or subscription selections.
  • Payment Information: Payment is processed through Razorpay. We do not store your credit card, debit card, or bank account details on our servers. We store only the payment reference and outcome returned by Razorpay.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, push notification tokens, and mobile network information for app functionality and security.
  • Content You Create: Reviews and ratings you post about a kitchen or dish, and messages you send in in-app chat to a home kitchen or to our support team.
  • Voice Input: If you use voice ordering (the "MEAL AI" assistant), the audio you speak is transmitted to our speech-to-text provider to produce a text query. Raw audio is not stored after transcription.
  • Support Records: If you raise a support ticket, the contents of that ticket and any attachments you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your food orders
  • Connect you with local home kitchens and delivery partners
  • Send order updates, confirmations, and delivery notifications
  • Improve our services, app functionality, and user experience
  • Communicate with you about promotions, offers, and app updates (with your consent)
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only as follows:

  • With Kitchens: Your name, order details, and delivery address are shared with the home kitchen preparing your food.
  • With Delivery Partners: Your name, delivery address, and phone number are shared with the delivery partner assigned to your order for delivery purposes.
  • With Payment Processors: Payment information is processed securely through Razorpay. We do not have access to your full card or bank details.
  • Artificial Intelligence Processors: Voice ordering is transcribed by Sarvam AI, and chat queries are answered by Google Gemini. These processors receive only the input required to produce a response. API keys for both are held on our servers and are never present in the Apps. Neither processor is used to train models on your data under our account.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): Push notification delivery involves your device push token, message content, and the timestamp of delivery.
  • For Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies and Analytics

The Apps do not use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers. We do not embed third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Connectivity and local preference data are stored on your device only and are not transmitted to us.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL. The Apps do not permit unencrypted ("cleartext") connections.
  • Secure storage of personal information on access-controlled servers
  • Authentication tokens and session secrets stored in the device's encrypted keystore
  • Access controls limiting who can access your data
  • Regular security assessments and updates

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant Data Protection Board without undue delay.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. We may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes — for example, order and payment records, which we are generally required to keep for accounting and tax purposes. When you delete your account, we will delete or irreversibly anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you, and receive a copy of it
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request erasure (deletion) of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent, at any time, for processing that relies on consent
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for location data at any time through your device settings
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@mealin.in. We respond to verified requests within 30 days. You may withdraw microphone permission for voice ordering at any time through your device settings; without it, text and menu browsing remain fully available.

8. Children's Privacy

MealIn is not intended for use by children under the age of 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. MealIn involves ordering food, arranging payment and sharing precise location with third parties, none of which we consider appropriate for children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

10. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute relating to it is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka.
